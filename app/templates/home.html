How it works

🍲

1. Providers list food

A canteen or hostel posts surplus food with quantity, expiry time and pickup location.

🤝

2. Verified NGOs request

Admin-approved NGOs request the food. The most urgent and nearest needs get priority.

✅

3. Food is collected

The provider accepts a request, the NGO collects the food, and our Impact page shows meals saved.

Built for three kinds of people

Providers

List leftover food in a minute and see who is coming to collect it.

NGOs

Find nearby food ranked by urgency, distance and your capacity.

Admins

Verify every NGO so food only goes to trusted organisations.
